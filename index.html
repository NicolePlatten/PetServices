Two ways we care

Whichever suits your household, we come to it.

Every booking happens on your ground — your house, your paddock, your routine. You choose how much company your animals need while you're away.

Service 01

Overnight & extended home stays

A dedicated sitter moves into your home for the length of your trip, keeping your animals on their normal routine, in familiar surroundings, day and night.

  • One sitter, one household — no shared shifts
  • Feeding, walking and litter/stable routines maintained exactly as you leave them
  • Post, plants and lights kept looking lived-in
  • Daily photo & written update
Service 02

Scheduled home visits

For shorter absences, we call in at agreed times to feed, exercise, muck out or simply keep your animals company — without anyone staying overnight.

  • Visits from 30 minutes to a full afternoon
  • Once daily, twice daily, or a full turnout-to-stable round for horses
  • Flexible, one-off or recurring bookings
  • Same photo & written update after every call

How a booking works

From first message to a safe return home.

01

Get in touch

Tell us your dates, your animals and your address. We'll confirm a sitter is available nearby.

02

Meet & greet

Your sitter visits beforehand to meet the animals, walk the property or yard, and note the routine.

03

Care, logged

Every visit or overnight stay is followed by a photo and a written note, so you always know how things are.

04

Home, as you left it

Animals settled, house or yard tidy, and a full handover note waiting for you when you're back.

Not every sitter covers a yard

Horses, cared for by people who know a yard.

Horses aren't an add-on for us — sitters taking on equine bookings are matched by hands-on experience, not just willingness. That means someone confident with turnout, stabling and the routine your horse already knows.

  • Turnout and bring-in on your usual schedule
  • Mucking out, hay, feed and water rounds
  • Rug changes and basic grooming
  • Comfortable working alongside livery yards and DIY setups
